## Purpose

This site is the association's management software (GVV). It is used to manage members, flights, training, billing and accounting. Access is restricted to members of the association and to people it authorises.

## Data collected

- Identity and contact details: name, first name, address, email, phone, date and place of birth, photograph.
- Financial data: amounts due, payments, bank details when required.
- Flying activity: licences, ratings and their validity dates, flight hours, training sessions.
- Medical information required by aviation regulations (validity dates).

## Purposes of processing

This data is used to manage membership, bill flights, follow pilots' training and recent experience, communicate with members, and meet the association's obligations towards federations and aviation authorities.

## Recipients

Access to data is limited according to each person's role in the association (board, treasurers, instructors…). Some data may be passed on to the aviation federations the association belongs to and to the competent authorities, when regulations require it.

Data is never sold or passed on for commercial purposes.

## Retention period

Data is kept for the duration of membership, then for as long as needed to meet the association's legal obligations, in particular accounting obligations.

## Security

Exchanges with the site are encrypted. Each user has a personal, password-protected account and only sees the information needed for their role.
